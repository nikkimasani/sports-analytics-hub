Import your fantasy league data from ESPN or Yahoo

ESPN Fantasy

Enter your ESPN league ID to import rosters, standings, and matchup data.

Find your League ID in your ESPN Fantasy URL:
fantasy.espn.com/football/league?leagueId=12345678
The number after "leagueId=" is your League ID. Works with public leagues only.
